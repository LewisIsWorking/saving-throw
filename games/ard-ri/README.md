# Ard Rí: The High King

Roll a d100. Whatever comes up is a **card you keep**: pick one of its two options and it goes into
your hand. Many cards are **powers** you play later, and some let you **steal** a power from
someone else's hand. How high you roll tells you how good the card is.

**Players:** 3+  
**You need:** a d100 (or two d10s), drinks, or the ComeOnOverUno app, which rolls, keeps every
hand and plays the powers for you.

## The unit

Every number is **sips**.

## Powers

A power card stays in your hand until you play it. Anyone may play a power at any time, and doing
so does not pass the phone.

| Power | Play it to |
|---|---|
| **Ward** | Cancel drinks aimed at you |
| **Summons** | Make anyone drink (the card says how much) |
| **Turning** | Change the direction of play |
| **Raid** | Steal one power card from anyone's hand |

Only power cards can be stolen. Ordinary cards are the record of what you rolled.
