# Horse Race

A card-based betting game for any number of players. One deck, four horses,
and a track that punishes you for backing the favourite.

**Players:** 2+ (works best with 4 or more, so the suits get spread around)
**You need:** a standard 52-card deck, drinks
**Length:** 5–10 minutes per race

---

## Setup

1. **Pull the four aces** out of the deck and lay them in a **vertical column**.
   These are the horses. Each ace is one lane: ♠ ♥ ♦ ♣.

2. **Build the track.** Deal **5–7 cards face down** in a **horizontal line**,
   running to the right of the aces. Each of these is a **checkpoint**.
   Fewer checkpoints means a shorter, swingier race; more means a longer one.

3. **Shuffle the rest** of the deck and set it face down as the draw pile.

```
        checkpoint 1   2      3      4      5
  ♠ A     [ ? ]      [ ? ]  [ ? ]  [ ? ]  [ ? ]
  ♥ A
  ♦ A
  ♣ A
   ^
   horses start here
```

4. **Everyone draws one card** from the deck. That card is your betting slip:
   - its **suit** is the horse you are backing
   - its **number** is your **stake**, in drinks

   Keep your card in front of you - you will need the number at the end.
   Players can and will end up on the same horse; that is fine.

---

## Running the race

Flip cards off the top of the deck, one at a time.

- The flipped card's **suit** moves that horse **one step to the right**.
- **The number on a flipped card is ignored.** Deck flips are movement only.
  Numbers matter only on your betting slip and on checkpoints.

Keep flipping. The horses jostle up and down the track as their suits come up.

### Checkpoints

A checkpoint stays face down until **all four horses have passed it**. The
moment the last horse clears it, **flip it face up**:

> Everyone backing **that card's suit** drinks **that card's number**.

So the checkpoints only ever bite once the whole field has moved on - the
race has to fully commit before the track collects.

### Winning

The first horse to move **past the final checkpoint** wins. With 5 checkpoints
that is 6 matching flips; with 7, it is 8.

- **Backers of the winning horse hand out their stake.** You have your card's
  number in drinks to give away, split however you like.
- **Everyone else drinks their own number.**

Back the winner and you are pouring. Back a loser and you are drinking.

---

## Card values

| Card | Value |
|------|-------|
| 2–10 | Face value |
| J, Q, K | **11** |
| A | Not applicable - the aces are the horses |

Elevens apply **everywhere a number counts**: your stake, and checkpoint cards.

---

## House calls

The rules above leave a few things open. Settle these before you start, not
mid-race:

- **Open or hidden slips?** Showing your card makes the race louder; hiding it
  makes the finish a bigger reveal.
- **Past vs. onto the last checkpoint.** Above, a horse must move *beyond* the
  final checkpoint. Requiring only that it *reach* the last checkpoint shortens
  each race by one flip.
- **Deck runs out?** Reshuffle the used flips (not the track, not the aces) and
  keep going. With 5 checkpoints this almost never comes up.
- **Checkpoint setback.** A common variant: when a checkpoint flips, that suit's
  horse also moves **back one step** as well as its backers drinking. This makes
  races much longer and much meaner. Off by default here.
